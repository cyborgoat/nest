# Explorer and Knowledge Packs

## Add a pack

- [Install from Hub](import-from-hub.md) — packs published by your team.
- [Import from a folder](import-from-folder.md) — Markdown already on your
  computer.
- [Import from a ZIP](import-from-zip.md) — a portable pack archive.

Nest keeps one installed version per pack ID.

## Browse and read

Explorer shows installed packs and their files. Open a Markdown file to read
it; open files appear in tabs.

## Update, export, remove

- **Update** installs a newer Hub release.
- **Export** creates a portable ZIP of the pack.
- **Remove** deletes the local copy (the Hub project and its releases stay).

## Chat visibility (optional)

Activate or deactivate a pack from its actions. Active packs are searched by
Chat; inactive packs stay installed and readable. This never deletes content.
