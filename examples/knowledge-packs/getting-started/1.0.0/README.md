# Getting Started with Nest

Welcome to Nest, a local-first workspace for collecting, reading, editing, and
sharing Markdown knowledge packs.

## Get knowledge into Nest

Start with [First 10 minutes](guides/first-10-minutes.md), then choose the guide
that matches where your knowledge is now:

- [Connect to your team's Hub](guides/settings-and-account.md), then
  [find and install a published pack](guides/import-from-hub.md)
- [Bring in Markdown from a folder](guides/import-from-folder.md)
- [Import a pack shared as a ZIP](guides/import-from-zip.md)

Your team must provide the Hub URL. A Hub connection is needed to browse the
shared catalog, but not to import a local folder or ZIP.

After installing or importing, use
[Explorer and knowledge packs](guides/library-and-packs.md) to open, organize,
update, export, or remove packs.

## What you will learn

You will learn how to find shared knowledge, bring local Markdown into Nest,
read and manage installed packs, and share your own knowledge by ZIP or Hub.
Editing, publishing review, and Chat are covered when you need them.

This pack is installed automatically on first launch. You may remove it like
any other pack; Nest will not reinstall it after you intentionally remove it.
