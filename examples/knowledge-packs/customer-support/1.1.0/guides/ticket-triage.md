# Ticket Triage Guide

## Intake

Capture:

- Account/workspace id
- Environment (desktop/hub)
- Severity and business impact
- Reproduction steps

## Classification

- Bug
- How-to question
- Feature request
- Outage/incident

## SLA routing

| Severity | First response | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Sev-1 | 15 min | Immediate |
| Sev-2 | 1 hour | 2 hours |
| Sev-3 | 1 business day | As needed |
